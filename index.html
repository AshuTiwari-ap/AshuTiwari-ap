About School

School History

S.D.S Convent School was founded to deliver affordable quality education in Gopalpur. Over the years, the school has built a strong reputation for academics, discipline, and all-round development.

Mission & Vision

Our mission is to shape responsible citizens with knowledge and values. Our vision is to create a future-ready generation with confidence, creativity, and compassion.

Infrastructure

Smart classrooms, library, science lab, computer lab, activity rooms, playground, CCTV enabled campus, and secure transport support create a safe learning environment.
