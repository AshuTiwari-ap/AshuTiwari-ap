Company History

Balaji Refiners began with a simple promise: shudh sarso ka tel.

Founded to serve Indian households and local retailers, Balaji Refiners combines traditional mustard oil preference with hygienic procurement, filtered storage, and branded retail packaging.

The website presents the company as an e-commerce-ready oil brand with distributor enquiries, customer ordering, order tracking, and admin operations.
